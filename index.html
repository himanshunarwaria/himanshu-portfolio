About

Design is communication. Code makes it tangible.

I’m Himanshu Narwaria, a multidisciplinary designer and developer with 5+ years of experience creating brand systems, campaigns, e-commerce design, and digital products. I currently work as Brand Manager at Gourmet Baklava and previously worked with GlobalBees and NVD.

My toolkit includes Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, Premiere Pro, After Effects, and modern frontend development. I care about the entire chain—from what a brand means to how the final interface behaves.
