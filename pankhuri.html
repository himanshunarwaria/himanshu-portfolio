The Brief

Pankhuri is a restaurant brand, and restaurant websites fail for one predictable reason: they bury the two things people came for — the menu and the way to book. This build is being designed around appetite first: warm photography-led layouts, a menu that reads beautifully on a phone, and a booking path that never hides. The full case study, with live screens, will be published here at launch.
