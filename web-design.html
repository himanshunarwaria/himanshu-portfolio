The process

How we’ll work together

  1. Map the purpose

    We identify the audience, business goal, pages, content, and the actions the website should make possible.

  2. Design around the content

    Wireframes establish structure before the visual direction is developed into responsive page layouts.

  3. Review the complete journey

    We check key paths, small-screen layouts, states, and handoff details before the design moves into production.

What to include in your brief

Before we begin

Your questions, answered.

Do you design for mobile as well as desktop?

Yes. Responsive layouts are part of the design process, including navigation, content order, forms, and important interaction states.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We start by reviewing the current structure, content, and key journeys, then decide what should be retained, improved, or replaced.

Is web development included in web design?

They can be commissioned together or separately. Design defines the layout and behaviour; development turns the approved design into a working website. The proposal makes that distinction explicit.

Will the website design support SEO?

I plan clear page structure, readable content, descriptive headings, and useful internal links. Technical implementation, content quality, and ongoing search visibility also need attention during development and after launch.
