About This Project

What Meta Ads were created for Gourmet Baklava?

Luxury gifting and product conversion ads for Gourmet Baklava — India's first FSSC 22000 & Sattvik-certified Mediterranean dessert brand. Including Diwali and Eid gifting campaign creatives, artisan product showcases, certification trust-builders, and collection launch ads for Facebook and Instagram.

What made these ads premium and effective?

Rich golden color palettes, close-up artisan craftsmanship shots, premium gift packaging visuals, and certification callouts — creating an aspirational luxury purchase experience while clearly communicating the brand's quality assurance to drive gifting conversions.
