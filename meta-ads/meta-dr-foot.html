About This Project

What Meta Ads were created for Dr. Foot?

Conversion-focused Facebook and Instagram ads for Dr. Foot's specialized foot care products — including benefit-forward product ads, before-after comparison creatives, problem-solution format ads, and multi-format ad sets optimized for different campaign objectives from awareness to purchase.

What was the conversion approach?

Benefit-first messaging emphasizing pain relief, comfort, and clinical credibility — clean product visuals with clear CTAs, doctor-endorsed positioning, and before-after proof elements designed to build trust and convert health-conscious buyers looking for effective foot care solutions.
