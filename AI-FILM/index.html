01 / Campaign

Kavya Pay — five films for a Mumbai payments business.

A counter-service payments brand in Andheri West: credit-card bill payments, recharges, money transfers, card-to-bank. Five vertical films, 35 to 71 seconds, in Hinglish — narrative, brand and walkthrough cuts, sharing one cast and one world.

The job
Make a neighbourhood payments counter feel as safe and as modern as an app. Cast and world hold across all five films, so the set reads as one campaign, not five posts.
The approach
Problem first, product second. The narrative cuts open on someone stuck — a bill due, a transfer that won't go through. App screens sit inside the frame, never cut away to.
Scope
Five films, vertical 9:16. Script, voice, Hinglish and Devanagari titling, branded lower-thirds, feature end-cards and the compliance line.
Role
Concept through delivery, solo. Direction, production, edit, grade and sound — one point of accountability for the whole set.

06 / Process

How a project actually runs.

Controlled production, not prompting until something sticks. Seven stages, each with something to approve before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Brief & discovery

    Product, audience, market, where the film runs and what it has to achieve. What we're not making gets defined here too.

  2. 02

    Concept & direction

    Two or three routes, each with a stated visual direction, reference frames and a reason it should exist.

  3. 03

    Script & shot list

    Beats, timing, dialogue or voice-over, and a shot-by-shot breakdown — signed off before anything is generated.

  4. 04

    Previsualisation

    Key frames and blocking as moving images. Angle, wardrobe, location and grade get locked while changes are still free.

  5. 05

    Production

    Scene generation against the approved shot list, holding continuity across character, wardrobe, location and light.

  6. 06

    Edit, grade & sound

    Assembly, pacing, colour, music, sound design, captions and brand elements.

  7. 07

    Delivery & variations

    Masters in the formats your media plan needs, plus alternate openings and cut-downs for testing.
