The Brief

Padma Shree Travels runs private AC cabs out of Agra — pilgrimage circuits, Taj Mahal sightseeing, outstation routes. All of that business lived on phone calls. There was no way for a tourist planning from another city to find them, check a fare, or book. The brief I set with the owner: build a website that earns trust in ten seconds, gets found for the exact routes people search, and takes a booking without forcing anyone to install an app.

What I Delivered — All of It
01

Design

A confident dark hero with the promise up front — fixed fare, verified driver, 24/7. Route card system, trust strip, and a booking widget designed around how travellers actually decide.

02

Development

Hand-coded static frontend (no page builder), with a Node.js backend on Netlify Functions: Razorpay checkout, payment verification, booking records written to Google Sheets, and SendGrid confirmation emails.

03

Copywriting

Every word on the site, written for trust: "The fare we quote is the fare you pay." Route descriptions, tour itineraries, FAQ answers, and blog articles — all original.

04

SEO

27 dedicated route and tour landing pages targeting real search intent — "Agra to Jaipur taxi", "temple tour by cab" — each with unique titles, descriptions, and structured content. Plus 301 redirect hygiene.

05

Responsive

Mobile-first throughout: most bookings happen on a phone, so the booking widget, tap-to-call, and WhatsApp actions lead the mobile layout.

06

Deployment

Netlify static hosting + serverless functions on a custom domain, with Google Ads conversion tracking and UTM capture wired into the booking flow. I also built the owner a lightweight internal CRM page to manage bookings.

The Work, Live

Under the hood

  • Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS — no framework overhead, fast on cheap phones and slow networks
  • Netlify Functions (Node.js): createOrder + verifyPayment for Razorpay Standard Checkout
  • Verified bookings written to Google Sheets via service account — the owner’s live lead pipeline
  • SendGrid transactional emails confirm every booking automatically
  • Blog with route-linked CTAs feeding the booking funnel
  • Google Ads conversion tracking with UTM capture end to end
  • Internal CRM page for staff — bookings managed without any third-party subscription
Where It Stands

The site is live and taking real bookings — WhatsApp-first, no app, no advance payment for most trips.

Every major route the company drives now has its own page a traveller can actually find.

The owner gets each lead in a spreadsheet and an email the moment it happens — no dashboard to learn.
