About This Project

What is Pista House?

Pista House is India's legendary Hyderabadi culinary institution, founded in 1997. It is renowned for its GI-tagged Haleem and traditional Biryani, with 44+ stores across India and globally. The brand is a cultural icon of Hyderabad's Nizami food heritage.

What was the design strategy for Pista House social media?

The strategy blended rich Nizami heritage aesthetics with modern visual storytelling — festive campaign creatives for Ramadan and Eid, daily food showcases, product launch posts, and cultural celebration content that reinforces Pista House's iconic brand identity.

Which platforms were covered?

Creatives were designed for Instagram and Facebook — covering feed posts, stories, and festive campaign carousels to engage both local Hyderabadi audiences and Pista House's growing national customer base.
