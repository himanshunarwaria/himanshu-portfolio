About This Project

What is Nomme Kitchen & Coffee Theatre?

Nomme Kitchen & Coffee Theatre is Hyderabad's premier fusion dining destination located in Banjara Hills. The restaurant is known for its sophisticated Italian-Chinese cuisine served in an intimate, design-forward atmosphere that blends culinary artistry with a curated visual experience.

What was the social media design approach for Nomme?

The design strategy focused on editorial, atmosphere-led visuals — warm neutrals, intentional whitespace, and refined typography — to reflect the restaurant's upscale positioning and drive table reservations through Instagram storytelling.

Which platforms were designed for?

Creatives were designed primarily for Instagram including feed posts, stories, and carousels — all crafted to maintain visual consistency, build brand recognition, and invite engagement from Hyderabad's fine dining audience.
