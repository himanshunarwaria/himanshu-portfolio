About This Project

What is Odyana?

Odyana is a Bengaluru-based digital grocery marketplace that connects quality suppliers with consumers seeking fresh, reliable daily household essentials. The platform focuses on a user-centric experience — bringing a curated selection of grocery categories directly to customers' doorsteps.

What was the social media design strategy for Odyana?

The strategy focused on clean, appetizing product visuals and category showcases — building appetite for the app, driving first-time downloads, and communicating the freshness and convenience of Odyana's marketplace through Instagram-friendly D2C content.
