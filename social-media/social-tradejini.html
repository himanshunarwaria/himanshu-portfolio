About This Project

What is TradeJini and CubePlus?

TradeJini is a technology-driven stock broking firm, and CubePlus is their flagship trading platform offering retail investors a clean, modern interface for equity trading. The platform focuses on simplifying stock market participation for new and experienced retail investors alike.

What was the social media design strategy for TradeJini?

The design strategy focused on building trust and platform confidence — market insight infographics, CubePlus dashboard UI showcases, investor education carousels, and data visualization posts designed to attract retail equity investors and drive platform signups on LinkedIn.
