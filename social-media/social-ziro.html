About This Project

What is Ziro Learning?

Ziro Learning is a Bengaluru-based preschool that uses the HighScope Canada curriculum — an internationally acclaimed active participatory learning framework that empowers young children to initiate their own learning experiences through play, exploration, and daily routines.

What was the social media approach for Ziro Learning?

Content was designed to reassure and inspire parents — showcasing the HighScope curriculum in action, milestone moments, classroom activities, and expert early childhood insights. The visual style was playful, warm, and trust-building to drive preschool enrollment inquiries.
