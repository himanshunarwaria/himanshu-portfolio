About This Project

What is Navodayan Academy?

Navodayan Academy is Indore's premier coaching institute specializing in IIT-JEE and NEET preparation. The academy offers structured Gurukul residential programs where students live and study in a focused academic environment designed to build discipline, deep subject mastery, and examination readiness.

What was the social media goal for Navodayan Academy?

The primary goal was driving admission inquiries from parents of Class 9-12 students in Indore and Madhya Pradesh. Content was designed to communicate academic credibility, faculty expertise, Gurukul program benefits, and student result achievements — all in a structured, trust-building visual language.

Which platforms were used?

Facebook was the primary platform for parent-targeted reach, supplemented by Instagram for student-facing content — together creating a comprehensive social media presence for the academy's admissions marketing.
