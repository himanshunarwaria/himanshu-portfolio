About This Project

What is Gourmet Baklava?

Gourmet Baklava is India's first FSSC 22000 and Sattvik-certified Mediterranean dessert brand, specializing in handcrafted artisan baklava. The brand focuses on premium quality, luxury gifting, and bringing authentic Middle Eastern dessert culture to Indian consumers.

What was the social media design approach?

The design used rich golden color palettes, artisan craftsmanship showcases, and premium gifting aesthetics to position Gourmet Baklava as a luxury purchase and gifting choice across Diwali, Eid, Christmas, and corporate gifting occasions.

What does FSSC 22000 and Sattvik certification mean?

FSSC 22000 is an internationally recognized food safety system certification, while Sattvik certification guarantees that products meet stringent vegetarian and pure ingredient standards. These certifications were prominently featured in the social media creatives to build consumer trust.
