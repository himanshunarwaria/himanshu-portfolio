The scope

What I can help with

We agree on the deliverables, review rounds, and handoff before work begins.

Concept and storyboard

A visual treatment, shot sequence, and reference direction to agree on the story before generating footage.

Generated visual scenes

Selected AI-assisted shots developed around the approved direction, with review for visual continuity and distracting artefacts.

Editing and brand finishing

A coherent sequence with pacing, titles, sound, and supplied brand assets. Exact logos and text are added in the finishing stage.

Social and campaign versions

Exports in agreed formats, with alternate lengths or crops planned around the usable scenes.

The process

How we’ll work together

  1. Check the creative fit

    We decide which scenes benefit from generation and which need real photography, supplied footage, or conventional motion design.

  2. Approve a visual direction

    We review a treatment and sample frames, then refine selected shots rather than generating an unstructured collection of clips.

  3. Edit and check the result

    I assemble the approved scenes and check continuity, product representation, captions, and brand consistency before delivery.

What to include in your brief

Share the concept, audience, brand assets, product references, intended use, length, and deadline. Include any elements that must be reproduced exactly, such as packaging, lettering, or a real location.

Before we begin

Your questions, answered.

What kinds of AI videos can you help create?

Concept-led brand videos, stylised product stories, atmospheric campaign scenes, and short social visuals. The suitability of each idea is checked against the level of realism, continuity, and control required.

Can AI reproduce my product and logo exactly?

Generated footage can vary between shots. For exact product details, logos, or text, I use supplied assets and conventional editing or compositing, and discuss any remaining limitations before production.

Do AI videos replace a traditional shoot?

They can support some ideas, but real products, demonstrations, interviews, and documentary claims may need real footage. A mixed approach is often appropriate when accuracy matters.

What is included in an AI video quote?

The quote defines the concept, scene count, intended length, generation and review scope, editing, and exports. We also agree on the assets and permissions needed for the intended use.
