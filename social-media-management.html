The scope

What I can help with

We agree on the deliverables, review rounds, and handoff before work begins.

Content planning

Content themes and a publishing calendar connected to your audience, offers, launches, and available assets.

Design and captions

Brand-aligned posts, carousels, and captions, with reels or video editing included when specified in the scope.

Approval and scheduling

A shared review process and publishing schedule for the agreed channels. Content is approved before it goes live.

Reporting and iteration

A review of available platform metrics, such as reach, engagement, profile activity, and link clicks, to inform the next content cycle.

The process

How we’ll work together

  1. Audit the current presence

    We review the channels, audience, visual identity, recent content, and the business outcome the work should support.

  2. Plan and create in batches

    The calendar sets the direction for a reviewable batch of content. You provide factual checks and consolidated feedback before scheduling.

  3. Publish and learn

    Approved content follows the schedule. Reporting helps us decide what to repeat, revise, or test next.

What to include in your brief

Share your social profiles, brand guidelines, business goals, current offers, available photos or footage, and the person responsible for approvals. Publishing access and any response responsibilities are agreed during onboarding.

Before we begin

Your questions, answered.

How is social media management different from design?

Design focuses on producing the visual assets. Management can also include content planning, captions, scheduling, and reporting. The channels and responsibilities are set out in the scope.

Are ad spend and paid campaign management included?

Ad spend is separate. Performance ad creative can be included, while media buying, campaign optimisation, and account management need an explicitly agreed scope.

Will you reply to comments and direct messages?

Community management is discussed separately so response times, escalation rules, and access are clear. It is not assumed to be part of a content-only engagement.

Can you guarantee follower growth or sales?

No. Results depend on the offer, audience, budget, platform behaviour, and other factors. We agree on useful metrics and use the available data to improve the work.
