Case Study — UI + Social

Tradejini

Clean and data-driven financial design — making complex trading feel simple, trustworthy, and visually confident.

IndustryFinance & Trading
ScopeUI Design + Social
RoleUI/UX & Visual Design
Duration10 Weeks
The Challenge

Tradejini needed a visual identity that projected data-driven confidence — precise, trustworthy, and clean enough to instill financial confidence.

The existing brand presence felt too complex and overwhelming for new traders. Social media lacked consistency and the visual language didn't communicate the trustworthy financial positioning the brand deserved. We needed to bridge that gap without losing authenticity.

The Insight
In finance, clarity is trust. When a dashboard reads well, users trade with confidence.

Design Philosophy — Tradejini

Creative Direction

Data clarity with confident precision

We developed a visual system rooted in clean whites, confident blues, and data-first layouts, and typographic hierarchy. Every element serves a purpose — nothing is decorative, everything communicates.

The palette draws from sapphire blue, cloud white, and chart green. Typography pairs a refined serif for headlines with a clean sans-serif for body text.

Final Design Execution

Dashboard UI System

A complete Instagram grid system with feed posts, carousels, and story templates — all sharing the same typographic rhythm and color language. Designed for data clarity across all screen sizes.

Social Content

Extended the visual language to market updates, investor posts, financial infographics, and promotional banners. Every customer touchpoint reinforces the trustworthy financial positioning.
