The process

How we’ll work together

  1. Understand the message

    We review your audience, competitors, existing assets, and the decisions your customer needs to make.

  2. Set the visual direction

    I develop a focused design direction and explain the choices behind the type, colour, imagery, and layout.

  3. Build the system

    The approved direction becomes a working set of assets, checked for consistency across the agreed formats.

What to include in your brief

Share your existing logo or guidelines, the content to communicate, required formats, reference work, and the date you need the assets. If you are starting from scratch, describe the business and its audience.

Before we begin

Your questions, answered.

Can you work with my existing brand guidelines?

Yes. I can extend an existing identity across new formats or identify where the guidelines need more detail. A full rebrand is scoped separately from ongoing design production.

Do you provide editable design files?

Editable files can be included in the agreed handoff. We confirm source formats, font or stock licences, and how your team will reuse the designs before the project begins.

How is graphic design priced?

Pricing depends on the number of concepts, deliverables, formats, and review rounds. Send the asset list and timeline for a project-specific quote.

Can I hire you outside Agra?

Yes. I work remotely with businesses across India and worldwide, using a shared brief and scheduled design reviews.
